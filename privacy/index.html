Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026

Service Break sends you notifications about live tennis matches. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. It's short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible for your data

SERVICE BREAK LLC is the controller of the information described here. You can reach us at contact@service-break.com.

What we collect

No account, no name, no email. Service Break has no sign-up. The app creates an anonymous identity (a random ID from Google Firebase) so your settings can follow your device. We never know who you are.

Device and notification data. To deliver alerts we store: a random device identifier the app generates, your device's push-notification token (issued by Apple on iPhone and iPad, and by Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android), and your app settings — favorite matches, alert choices, quiet hours, snooze, and league filters — with timestamps.

Match data is public. Scores and match states shown in the app are public sports information; nothing about the matches comes from you.

Diagnostics. The app currently keeps a small technical log on your device only, to help us fix problems. It is not transmitted. If we ever enable in-app analytics, we will update this policy first.

Website visitors

We count visits to service-break.com using Plausible, a privacy-focused analytics service. No cookies are set and nothing is stored on your device. Plausible records the page visited, the site that referred you, your screen size, and a country derived from your IP address — the IP address itself is not kept, and none of this identifies you. The data is processed in the EU, is used only to understand how the site is used so we can improve it, is never shared for anyone else's purposes, and feeds no advertising.

Our basis for this is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used. You can object at any time — if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal we will not count your visit, and you can email us to object by any other means.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information. To anyone, ever.
  • We don't show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, or photos.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who processes data for us

Five service providers handle data solely to run the app and this website: Google Firebase (anonymous sign-in and push delivery), Apple (push delivery to iPhone and iPad), Expo (push delivery, and a check for app updates each time the app opens), Render (our server and database hosting), and Plausible (website visit counts). Each receives only what its job requires.

Where your data goes

We are based in the United States and our app's providers are US companies, so information covered by this policy is transferred to and stored in the United States. Where the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR applies to you, those transfers are made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum) in our agreements with those providers. Website visit counts are the exception — Plausible is an EU company and processes that data on EU infrastructure.

Retention and deletion

Settings live as long as your device uses the app. Deleting the app abandons your anonymous identity; the associated data becomes unreachable and is removed in routine cleanup. To request earlier deletion, email us with your request — since we can't identify you, we'll tell you what to send from the app so we can find the right record.

Your rights

Where the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR applies to you, you have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, to object to our use of it under legitimate interests, and to receive it in a portable form. Depending on where you live, similar rights may apply under other laws, including US state privacy laws.

One honest limit: we cannot identify you from your data. There is no account and no name attached to anything we hold, so to act on a request we need you to send the identifier shown in the app. Email us and we will tell you where to find it. If we cannot link a request to a record, we are not able to act on it.

You can also complain to a regulator. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In the EU it is the supervisory authority in the country where you live or work. You do not have to contact us first, though we would rather you did.

Your choices

Notifications can be turned off any time in the app or in your device's Settings. Everything the app stores about you exists only to deliver the alerts you asked for.

Age

Service Break is for adults. It is not intended for or directed at anyone under 18, or under whatever higher age counts as an adult where you live. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone below that age. If you believe a minor has used the app, email us and we will delete the associated record.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app.
